# FROSTLINE — the storyboard

The finished demo's whole path, screen by screen. This is the bar the repair rungs land against: every displayed number has code behind it, every screen has one door forward, and no door lies. Where the live game already meets a line, the audit says so; everything else is the target.

---

## Screen 1 — THE CONTRACT BOARD

The bare load's screen, and the screen every finished job returns to.

- The books at the top: the purse, the heat, the roster.
- Three jobs. Each shows: its name — and the name is its mission type, really built (escort, hold, clear, reach); its posted pay; CLEAN or UNDERGROUND; its heat cost; HOT ROUTE when the way there is ambushed.
- A taken job leaves the board. A board with no jobs left refreshes.
- The address names the board. Reload brings back this board with its remaining jobs — never a skirmish, never a fresh roll.

## Screen 2 — THE ROUTE (hot jobs only)

- The ambush cannot be skipped: the job routes through it, not a link around it.
- Three ships against three. Free flight until first contact; contact freezes time into turns.
- Orders priced like the ground: MOVE is a capped burn, the cap shown; ATTACK shows the chance to hit; three points a ship; your half ends when your ships are done or the clock caps.
- Kills pay ship bounties into the one purse.
- Lost: the contract dies and the loss costs something real. Won: CONTINUE TO THE JOB.
- The route rides the tape like the ground does.
- Camera: zoom and pan, phone and desktop.

## Screen 3 — THE GROUND BATTLE

- The mission the job named, on the contract's own seed, fielding the roster's living men. A reload mid-battle comes back to the same contract battle.
- The objective visible from the first frame — on screen or pointed at from the edge.
- Free move until first contact. Then turns: three points a squad, and the points buy what the words say — orders that stack, or an economy priced at what one order really is.
- MOVE shows the cover there, the distance, the cap. ATTACK shows a real chance to hit — only for shots the trigger will really take. HOLD shows the cover here. OVERWATCH is priced right every turn: one point the narrow cone, a second point this turn the wide one. MARK feeds a rule the guns obey. DISCIPLINE as shipped.
- ENEMY TURN holds your side; YOUR TURN holds the enemy. One side moves at a time, as the banner says.
- Time stops on contact, on a man down, on orders done.
- THE HUNTER moves like his words or wears words that match his code.
- The battle ends in a win card or a loss card — never a crash, whatever the roster.

## Screen 4 — THE DEBRIEF

- The books: bounties, the posted price, the purse, the roster, the fallen this battle and the campaign's dead.
- Heat that something reads — a price, a hunter, a refusal — or no heat at all.
- The tape, with a door: WATCH THE REPLAY plays this battle back.
- REPLACEMENTS and the shop: every button that lights does what it says; an owned one-of-a-kind stays dark.
- THE BOARD returns to the board this job came from, one job lighter.

## Screen 5 — THE ENDINGS

- Broke and wiped: a campaign-over card — earned, kills, the dead — and NEW CAMPAIGN.
- RESET asks before it wipes.

## The spine

One address is always one exact state. Every number on screen has a mechanism behind it. Every screen has one door forward. No door lies.
